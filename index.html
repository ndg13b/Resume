What I Do

Modeling & statistics

Regression models built from the ground up across a decade of research. At Safety National it is the other side of that work — running, adjusting, and validating established gradient-boosting models whose output feeds underwriting and claims questions.

Getting the data usable

Pulling and joining from large, complex sources in Microsoft SQL Server, then cleaning and reshaping in Python. Most of the value in an analysis is decided before any model is fit.

Designing the question

A decade of experimental design and statistical inference, applied to figuring out what a stakeholder is actually asking — and what the data can and cannot say about it.

Explaining it

Reports, dashboards, and presentations for people who need the conclusion, not the method. Three years of running undergraduate lab sections, plus published papers and conference talks, is good practice for finding the right level.

adherence

Python

Scores how regular a behavior is — not just how often it happens — from nothing but a list of timestamps, then forecasts when the next session will land. Because it only ever sees timestamps, the same model applies to medication timing, exercise, physiotherapy, or a training program.
